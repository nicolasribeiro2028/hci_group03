- **Deliverables:**

  - 1 PDF Supplementary Materials: interview scripts & interview summaries

- **Deliverables:**

  - 1 PDF Prototype Report: 2 low-fi prototypes & storyboards (clear workflow)

- **Release:** Oct 5, 2026 · **Start:** Oct 9, 2026 · **Due:** Oct 29, 2026, 11:59 PM

  - Blog update

  - 1 PDF User Study Report: full report of the final user study
  - 1 High-Fidelity Prototype: shown on Demo Day
  - 1 Final Video: project's journey from needfinding through hi-fi prototype to conclusion
  - Blog update
- **Release:** Nov 2, 2026 · **Start:** Nov 6, 2026
- **Demo Day:** Dec 15, 2026, 13:00

  - 15% Study report
  - 15% Final video presentation

## Grading philosophy (what matters)
- Most important: **process** and having a **functional prototype** at each stage, with clear design rationale.
- Project must primarily address an **HCI / interaction / interface design problem** — not building LLMs, system prompts, AI pipelines, or the underlying technology itself.
- Not a big problem: arriving close to existing solutions, or negative/unexpected study results.
- A problem: missing/misapplied/undocumented methods, design decisions not grounded in findings, a mainly-technical contribution, or an infeasible scope that blocks finishing deliverables.

## AI usage policy
- **Allowed with transparency:** brainstorming, idea generation, summaries, exploring alternatives. Always acknowledge AI use; critical reflections on AI's role are encouraged in the blog.
- **Not a substitute for learning:** critique and adapt AI outputs, don't copy directly — the exercises are for your own practice.
- **Prohibited:** submitting AI-generated reports/code without your own contribution; fabricating data (fake interviews/results).
