# A.0 Declaration of Use of AI

We used AI in this report in the following ways.

- **Finding related work.** We described our needs and key ideas to AI assistants (Claude through Claude Code, ChatGPT and Perplexity), which searched the web for related papers, products and articles.
- **Organizing and synthesizing reading.** We used Claude (through Claude Code) to summarize sources, compare multiple articles and identify the most relevant ones for each need. The paper summaries were based on abstracts and web search results, not full texts, and the product descriptions on documentation and reviews, not hands-on use.
- **Writing Markdown.** We used Claude Code and the AI agent in Cursor to organize our notes and write them as Markdown files, including the structure of this report.
- **Figures and layout.** We used the AI agent in Cursor to crop the affinity diagram into readable parts and to set up the LaTeX layout of the PDF.
- **Dictation.** Some team members used Wispr Flow, a dictation tool, to type faster.

The research questions, the choice of baselines, the needs and the conclusions are the team's. The team reviewed and edited the AI-assisted text.
