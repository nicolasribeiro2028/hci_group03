### S3: TeachLM

- Tested Anthropic's Learning mode, OpenAI's Study Mode and Google's Guided Learning by hand.
- Reports that all three spend practically no time establishing the learning context: the existing level of understanding, the learning goal or the student's motivation.

## Need 4: Support that keeps students doing the thinking

### S8: The Rundown post

- The author uses AI with a framework that first builds context about the problem and how much the person knows. The AI does not produce solutions directly but gathers understanding, then guides the user.
- Meta-information: shows how people are approaching AI as a partner in guiding how to discuss or complete tasks, not just as an answer generator. It is a community post about writing and decision-making, not research, so it is context and inspiration rather than evidence.

### S9: Claude Code learning output style

- The mode does not perform everything. It leaves TODOs in the templates so the user makes decisions. The docs say it also applies to design decisions (`/output-style learning`).
