# Lo-fi Prototyping

Lo-fi prototyping is the iterative generation of artifacts that answer questions and bring us closer to the final solution. The output is one PDF, **Lo-fi Prototyping Report**, written in [lofi-prototyping-report.md](lofi-prototyping-report.md).

## Task

1. Two interactable lo-fi prototypes realizing our ideas
   - Each covers the main interactive sequences of the concept and has a mechanism for interaction (clickable areas, slide transitions, digital or manual interactions).
   - Allowed forms: paper sketches, wireframes, rough diagrams, simple digital mockups. Google Slides or Wizard of Oz can simulate interaction.
   - No full application and no focus on visual aesthetics.
   - Capture the core features and main workflow for peer testing.
   - The workflow must be complete enough for users to explore freely, not just follow one predefined path.
2. Two interaction storyboards, one per prototype (slides 19 to 21 of `HCI26-Week03-2-Prototype.pdf`)
   - Interaction storyboards, **not** concept storyboards.
   - Each illustrates a specific interaction sequence that leads the user to achieve a particular goal or task, chosen to best represent the core functionality.
   - Make clear who the user is, what they want to accomplish and how the prototype supports it.

## Report sections

- **B.0 Declaration of Use of AI:** short declaration of which AI tools were used and how. If none were used, say so explicitly.
- **B.1 Lo-fi Prototypes:** for each prototype, a 5 to 10 sentence summary (core idea, main workflow(s), testing purpose), screenshots or photos of the key screens shown in the storyboard, and a link to the file with the complete workflow.
- **B.2 Interaction Storyboards:** one image or diagram per storyboard, labeled "Storyboard – Idea A" and "Storyboard – Idea B", with the contextual story of how the user's goal is accomplished.

## Submission

- One PDF named "Lo-fi Prototyping Report" (here: `g3-lofi-prototyping-report.pdf`).
- Two separate prototype files (PowerPoint, Google Slides, pictures, Figma link or PDF) showing the entire workflow to be peer-tested, in `Deliverables/LoFiPrototyping/IdeaA/` and `Deliverables/LoFiPrototyping/IdeaB/`.
- Show both prototypes to the TA during the exercise session before the deadline.
- Official location: `Deliverables/LoFiPrototyping`.
