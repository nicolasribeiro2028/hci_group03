# Interview Script Template

**Topic:** When is help actually helpful? (Studying with AI)
**Estimated duration:** 20–30 min

## Intro (2 min)
- Introduce yourselves and the project (no need to reveal the specific HCI angle you're probing, to avoid biasing answers).
- Explain the interview is for a course project, will be anonymized, and ask for consent to take notes / record.

## Warm-up (3 min)
- Can you walk me through how you typically study for a course/exam?
- What tools do you currently use (apps, AI tools, notes, etc.)?

## Core questions (15 min)
- Tell me about the last time you used an AI tool (e.g., ChatGPT) while studying. Walk me through what happened.
- What were you hoping to get out of that interaction?
- Did you feel like you learned something, or just got an answer? How could you tell the difference?
- Have you ever noticed AI use backfiring — e.g., you couldn't reproduce a solution later? Tell me about that.
- (If working in groups) How do you use AI when studying with others? What goes well / poorly?
- How do you currently decide when to ask AI for help vs. figure something out yourself?
- If you could change one thing about how AI tools support your studying, what would it be?

## Closing (3 min)
- Is there anything else about your experience with AI and studying you think is important that we haven't covered?
- Thank participant, explain next steps.

## Notes for interviewer
- Ask "why" and "tell me more" liberally — avoid leading questions.
- Probe for concrete stories/examples rather than general opinions.
