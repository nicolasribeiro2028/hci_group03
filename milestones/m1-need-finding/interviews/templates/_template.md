# Post-Interview Reflection

## Participant snapshot

- Major and year:
- Course difficulty (1 to 5):
- Asks for help quickly or tries alone first:

## 1. Main takeaways
In two or three sentences, what stood out most about how this person gets help?

## 2. What made help useful for them?
Include the criteria they used, such as feeling they understood, speed, tone, or still doing the work themselves.

## 3. What made help unhelpful?
Note any moments where help was insufficient, too much, or confusing.

## 4. Sources of help
Which sources did they mention (friend, TA, professor, video, forum, textbook, AI tool)? Did the source change how it went?

## 5. Study methods
How did their approach differ across solving problems, exam prep, and learning in general?

## 6. Best quotes
Write 2 or 3 quotes close to word for word, with a note on why each matters.

## 7. Surprises
What did you not expect, or what contradicted our assumptions?

## 8. Link to our goal
What does this suggest about how to keep people from using AI in a way that makes them learn less?

## 9. Follow-ups
What would you ask next time, or what is unclear and worth checking with other participants?

## 10. How the interview went
Which questions worked well, and which were confusing or skipped? Did anything I said influence their answers? Anything to change in the outline?
